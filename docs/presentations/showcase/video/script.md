# Audiobook Manager - showcase script (~92s)

Source: article.md (project brief) + showcase/index.html (slide 2) + git log (467 commits). English.

---

Audiobooks are a mess. Too many separate platforms, none of which support good old raw audio files. There's no way to unify your library.

---

So I built one. Point Audiobook Manager at your audiobook files and it does the rest: reads the files, fetches covers and descriptions from Open Library and Google Books, groups the series, and tracks playback positions for each and every book you listen to, so you can always pick up where you left off.

---

Books are displayed as covers, sorted on shelves by genre and series, with a convenient Continue listening row. Press play - a card opens showing the player, as well as the book's chapters, and information. Listening progress is synced via the remote server. Stop on your phone, carry on on your laptop. Everyone in the house gets their own profile; kids accounts set an age band, so inappropriate books don't show. On Android, books can be downloaded offline, so you can travel with the phone, listening position is saved locally and progress is synced when you're back.

---

The files stay read-only. It works with the internet off. Cover lookups online send book details only, never anything about your household. Data is protected under the GDPR retention policy - erase and export on request. Shipped September to November, about 470 commits.
