# Product video — phone screen recording: model notes

Companion notes for the screen recording used in the product video. One continuous take, recorded on an Android phone on the home network. The app is shown as it ships — nothing is staged.

Two servers appear in the take:

- **The production server** — where the app starts.
- **A locally hosted server** — a second, home server used to show switching libraries. Servers you've connected to are remembered, so reconnecting is one tap.

## The flow

1. **Sign in with Google.**
2. **Land on the Library screen.**
3. **Open a book from "Continue where you last left off."**
4. **Download the book to your device.**
5. **Open the Discover tab** — recommendations based on your favourite books.
6. **Tap a recommendation** to see links on where to buy it — some books also have free LibriVox links.
7. **Go to the Settings tab.**
8. **Connect to the locally hosted server** — server history is saved, making reconnecting one tap.
9. **Sign into your local account with your password.**
10. **Enable fingerprint sign-in.**
11. **Sign in with your fingerprint.**
12. **As you can see, the app has merged the local server's library with the books you've downloaded on your phone** — all the books you want are still here and playable.
13. **Open that downloaded book from earlier.**
14. **Download a book from the local server's library.**
15. **Check the Settings page** to see the updated total download size of all your downloaded books.
16. **See a book you don't want anymore? Remove it from your library.**
17. **As you can see, it no longer shows up.**
18. **Connect back to the production server.**
19. **Your listening progress is synced back to the production server.**
20. **You can still listen to the book you downloaded from the local server** — books you didn't download from the local server no longer appear.
21. **Thanks for watching.**

## What the recording shows

- Signing in with Google, password or fingerprint (you enable fingerprint in the take)
- "Continue where you last left off" — playback resumes from your saved position
- Downloading books for offline listening, and your total download size in Settings
- A merged library — the server's catalogue and your phone's downloads in one shelf
- Discover recommendations based on your favourite books, with links on where to buy (including free LibriVox links where available)
- Reconnecting to a previous server in one tap from Settings
- Removing a book you don't want from your library — it disappears
